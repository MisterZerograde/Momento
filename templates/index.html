👤 USER GUIDE — READ THE DASHBOARD

This guide walks you through every panel, chart, color and button in plain English. No math here — formulas live in the Technical Reference tab above.

The dashboard analyzes XAUUSD (gold) 1-hour candles in Bangkok time (UTC+7). Use it to plan when to trade gold during the week and how much price typically moves.

⚡ Quick Start (60 seconds) 🎨 Color & Badge Legend 🔄 Top Bar — Refresh & Auto-fetch 📋 Sidebar Navigation ◉ Overview Panel ▦ Heatmap Panel ◔ By Hour Panel ◑ By Day Panel ★ Top Slots Panel ⚙ Settings Panel 🚀 Recommended Workflow

⚡ QUICK START — 60 SECONDS

  1. Look at the NOW Hero (big blue block at the top of Overview). It tells you what's happening right now: time, session, expected price movement this hour, and how confident the dashboard is.
  2. Glance at the Forecast Strip below it. Each card = one upcoming hour today. Big number = expected $ move. Green/red bar = direction tendency. Skip any card labelled SKIP or RESET.
  3. Open the Heatmap. Find dark blue cells — those are the most active (hour × day) slots. Switch to BULL/BEAR % to see direction tendency. Cross-check with Top Slots.
Cheat: The single best gold-trading window of the week (statistically) is row #1 in Top Slots after you apply RELIABILITY ★ and MIN SAMPLES ≥30 filters. That's the highest-edge slot that also passes statistical reliability.

🔄 TOP BAR — REFRESH & AUTO-FETCH

The strip at the top of every panel has these elements from left to right:

Meta info (left side)

Dataset summary: symbol (XAUUSD.sc), total bars loaded, date range (oldest → newest bar), and the timezone (always BKK · UTC+7).

"updated Ns ago" indicator

Time since the last successful MT5 fetch. Reads seconds → minutes → hours as time passes. Shows syncing… while a fetch is in progress. Turns red if 2 consecutive auto-fetches failed — you're trading on stale data and need to fix MT5.

AUTO ON / AUTO OFF toggle (pill button)

Green pulsing dot = ON. The dashboard silently re-fetches new MT5 bars every 60 seconds and refreshes all charts in the background. Default: ON. Your preference is saved in your browser.

Grey dot = OFF. No background fetching. You need to click the manual refresh button to pull fresh bars.

⟳ REFRESH MT5 button

Forces an immediate MT5 fetch and reloads everything. Useful after a news event or when you first open the dashboard. The button spins while the fetch is running (typically 3–10 seconds). If a fetch is already in progress, clicking again shows "Already fetching — please wait…".

Heads up: MetaTrader 5 must be running and logged in for fetches to work (manual or auto). If MT5 is closed, the status indicator turns red after 2 failed cycles.

◉ OVERVIEW PANEL

Your home page. Shows now + today + recent regime all in one view. You'll spend 90% of your time here.

1 · NOW Hero (blue gradient block at top)

Three cells. Each one answers a different "what's the situation?" question.

Left cell — Now · BKK

Current BKK time in big letters, plus the day of week, current trading session, and last gold price from your dataset. The session badge at the bottom has a colored dot:

  •  Green dot = market active, tradeable.
  •  Red dot = thin-market or dead hour — skip.
  •  Grey dot = weekend, market closed.
Middle cell — This hour typically

What the current hour looks like historically (averaged across the same DOW+hour combinations in your dataset). Three rows:

  • Avg Range — expected $ move from low to high in this hour.
  • Bull bias — % of past bars at this slot that closed green. 50% = coin flip.
  • Samples — how many past bars went into that average. More = more reliable.

Below those: a Confidence bar showing how much to trust the average. Empty bar = ignore; full bar = very reliable. (If this exact DOW+hour has too few samples, it falls back to "all days, this hour" — noted in italic.)

Right cell — Next high-vol window

Looks ahead the next 8 hours and finds the first slot whose expected range is >15% above the dataset average. Shows a countdown until that slot starts and what range to expect. If nothing qualifies, says "no high-vol slot in the next 8h".

2 · Stat Tiles (4 small boxes below the hero)

BEST HOUR

The single hour of day with the highest historical avg range (excluding thin/dead hours). This is the most active hour in an average week.

BEST DAY

The day of week with the highest average hourly range. Trade more aggressively on this day.

OVERALL BULL

Across all hours, % of bars that closed green. ~50% means gold is neither persistently bullish nor bearish over your dataset.

DATA COVERAGE

Number of bars loaded and how many years that covers. More years = more reliable averages.

3 · Regime Banner (colored bar)

Compares recent volatility to the long-term average. Tells you if the market is currently more or less active than usual.

  • 🔥 HOT REGIME — Recent moves are 25%+ larger than long-term. Widen stops. Avoid tight scalps.
  • ❄️ COOL REGIME — Recent moves are 15%+ smaller. Scale targets down. Breakout setups underperform.
  • 📊 NORMAL — Within ±15% of historical. Use standard sizing.

The "recent" window length is set in Settings → Regime Lookback (default 30 days).

4 · Today's Forecast Strip

One card for every remaining H1 hour today. Read left (now) to right (end of day).

  • Highlighted blue card = the current hour. Everything else = upcoming.
  • Session tag (top of card): Asia / Lon / OVL / NY — what session that hour belongs to.
  • Big $ number = expected range for that hour (historical avg at this DOW+hour).
  • Colored bar at the bottom = bull bias as a horizontal bar (green = bullish lean, red = bearish lean).
  • SKIP badge = thin-market or Monday-gap hour. Stats are unreliable — don't trade.
  • RESET badge = 4 AM dead hour (no bar will be produced by your broker).

Hover any card for exact numbers including bull %, samples, and confidence.

5 · Hourly Momentum chart (left)

Two layers on one chart:

  • Blue bars = historical avg range per hour (all-time).
  • Red line = recent N-day avg range per hour (N = your regime lookback).

How to read: Where the red line is above the blue bars, recent action is hotter than usual for that hour. Where it's below, recent action is cooler. Faint colored bands behind the chart = session boundaries (orange Asia / blue London / purple Overlap / green NY).

6 · Daily Efficiency chart (right)

Dotted line of average range by day of week. Helps you decide which weekdays to be aggressive (high range = wider opportunities) vs cautious (low range = tight action).

7 · Bull/Bear Bias by Hour chart (bottom)

Bar chart with the y-axis centered on 0 (representing 50% = neutral). Each bar = how far that hour's bull % deviates from a coin flip:

  • Bar above 0 (green) = historically closes more often green at this hour.
  • Bar below 0 (red) = historically closes more often red.
  • Tall bar in either direction = strong directional edge worth trading.

▦ HEATMAP PANEL

A 7-row × 24-column grid showing every day-of-week × hour-of-day combination at a glance. The fastest way to spot patterns.

How to read the grid

  1. Rows = days of week (Mon at the top, Sun at the bottom).
  2. Columns = hours of day in BKK (12 AM on the left → 11 PM on the right).
  3. Cell color = the value of the selected metric. Darker = higher (for Range / Move / Samples / Optimal). For Bull/Bear: green = bullish, red = bearish, white = 50/50 neutral.
  4. Cell number = the actual value, if SHOW VALUES is on. Click HIDE VALUES to clean up the view.
  5. 4 AM column = always empty grey (broker reset, no bar exists).

The text above the grid shows the global maximum and minimum cell for the current metric — quick orientation without scanning every cell.

The 5 metric buttons

AVG RANGE (default)
Expected $ movement per hour at each slot. Most useful for sizing trades.
MOVE %
Same as range, but as % of price. Better for comparing across years (gold's price doubled since 2022).
BULL/BEAR %
Directional bias per cell. Green = bulls win more often, red = bears. White cells = 50/50 (no edge). Look for cells with strong color.
SAMPLES
How many bars went into each cell. Pale cells = low sample count = low confidence. Use to discount weak signals.
OPTIMAL ★
Combined score: range strength × directional bias. Dark cells = both a big move AND a clear direction. The most actionable view.

SHOW VALUES / HIDE VALUES toggle

Top-right of the heatmap. Toggles the numeric labels in each cell on/off. Hide for a cleaner color-only view; show to read exact numbers.

Special-hour markers (above the grid)

Three colored dots in the legend bar tell you which columns have caveats — 2–3 AM (thin), 4 AM (dead), 5 AM Mon (gap). Details in Technical Reference → Special Hour Logic.

Reading caution: A dark range cell with very few samples is suspicious — could be noise from a single big day. Always cross-check with SAMPLES mode before trusting a cell.

🚀 RECOMMENDED TRADING WORKFLOW

  1. Open Overview. Check the Regime Banner — HOT = widen SL, COOL = scale TP down, NORMAL = standard sizing.
  2. Scan the Forecast Strip. Find the next high-range hour. Is it SKIP or RESET? If so, skip it.
  3. Confirm on Heatmap (AVG RANGE mode). Is the cell dark? Now switch to SAMPLES — does it have enough data?
  4. Check direction. Switch Heatmap to BULL/BEAR %. Is the cell clearly green or red? White = no directional edge.
  5. Cross-reference Top Slots. Is your hour in the top 20? Apply MIN SAMPLES ≥30 and RELIABILITY ★ to filter out noise.
  6. Size the trade. Use Avg Range × 0.65 for TP, Avg Range × 0.40 for SL → ~1.6:1 R:R.
  7. Verify data freshness. Check the top-bar "updated Xs ago". If red or many minutes old, click ⟳ REFRESH MT5.
Quick cheat sheet:
✅ Best windows: 19:00–23:00 BKK (Overlap) and 23:00–01:00 BKK (NY open)
❌ Avoid: 02:00–04:59 BKK (NY close + thin + broker reset)
⚠ Dead zone: 04:00 BKK (no bar generated)
⚠ Gap risk: Monday 05:00 BKK (weekend gap inflates the bar)
This dashboard is a statistical guide, not a signal generator. Every average is historical. Any single hour can move far outside its average due to news. Always use proper risk management.

📦 DATA SOURCE & COVERAGE

Where does the data come from?

Bars are fetched directly from MetaTrader 5 (Vantage International broker) using the symbol XAUUSD.sc on the H1 (1-hour) timeframe. The fetch script (fetch_data.py) pulls up to 50,000 bars and saves them to data/xauusd_1h.csv.

Each bar has: time (UTC), open, high, low, close, volume. The dataset currently spans ~4 years of hourly bars (~23,000+ rows).

Example bar:
time: 2024-03-15 14:00 UTC = 21:00 BKK
open: 2165.40 · high: 2172.80 · low: 2163.10 · close: 2170.50
→ Range = 2172.80 − 2163.10 = $9.70 · Bull bar (close > open)

How to update data

Click ⟳ REFRESH MT5 in the top-right. This runs fetch_data.py, reconnects to MT5, downloads new bars, and reloads all charts. Requires MT5 to be running and logged in.

🕐 TIME ZONE & TRADING SESSIONS

Why Bangkok time (UTC+7)?

All hours are converted from UTC to Asia/Bangkok (UTC+7, no DST) because it is a stable, DST-free timezone that aligns well with Asian session traders. Session boundaries never shift with daylight saving.

Trading Sessions in BKK Time

Asia 06–14 06:00–14:59 BKK — Tokyo + Sydney. Gold moves slowly unless there is Asian macro news. Spreads are moderate. Typically the lowest avg range of the day outside of dead hours.
London 14–19 14:00–18:59 BKK — European open drives significant gold flow. Range picks up sharply. Pre-US macro releases often produce spikes during this window.
Overlap 19–23 19:00–22:59 BKK — London + New York both open. Historically the highest-range window of the day. Major US data (NFP, CPI, FOMC) always drops here. The data confirms this is the strongest session for XAUUSD.
NY 23–04 23:00–03:59 BKK — Purely NY session after London closes. Ranges remain elevated early (23:00–01:00) then taper as NY approaches close. Hours 0 and 23 are the two highest-range hours in the dataset.
Note on DST: Although BKK itself has no DST, the US and Europe do. This shifts the session boundaries by ±1 hour seasonally. The dashboard uses fixed BKK-hour boundaries that represent the typical (EDT) season. During winter (EST), NY open is one hour later than shown.

⚠ SPECIAL HOURS — ALWAYS READ BEFORE TRADING

Thin Market · 2–3 AM BKK
What it is: NY session close + broker rollover window. Spreads widen significantly, liquidity disappears, and slippage is high.

In the data: These bars exist but have abnormally low or misleading ranges caused by widened spreads, not real price movement.

Action: Do not enter trades. The dashboard flags these with ⚠ NY Close and excludes them from clean stats.
Dead Hour · 4 AM BKK
What it is: Vantage MT5 performs its daily reset at broker midnight (UTC+3 00:00) = 4:00 AM BKK. The broker never generates an H1 bar for this hour — the candle simply does not exist.

In the data: Zero rows for BKK hour 4 across all ~4 years.

Action: No stats are possible. Shown as a gap in all charts. The 3 AM candle ends at 03:59, and the next candle starts at 05:00.
Monday Gap · 5 AM BKK Mon
What it is: The first H1 bar of the trading week (Monday 05:00 BKK ≈ Sunday night open). This bar includes the full weekend gap from Friday's close to Sunday's open.

In the data: The range is often inflated by the gap, not by real intraday movement.

Action: Stats for Mon 8 AM include the gap risk. The dashboard flags it and excludes it from "clean" stats used for Top Slots.
Weekend · Sat AM – Sun PM
What it is: XAUUSD H1 bars exist on early Saturday (the tail of Friday's NY session in UTC) and resume late Sunday (Asian open ~18:00 BKK). Saturday bars at 00:00–05:59 BKK are valid NY-session bars.

Action: Saturday midnight–6AM data is used normally. Saturday daytime and Sunday before 18:00 are closed — the dashboard shows a "Market Closed" state.
Summary rule: Avoid entering trades at 2–3 AM BKK (thin market) and treat 4 AM BKK as a hard gap. Do not rely on Monday 5 AM stats for scalp entries — the gap inflates the range.

📐 CORE METRICS & FORMULAS

All metrics are computed per H1 bar, then aggregated by hour-of-day and day-of-week. Dollar values are in USD per troy ounce — the unit in which XAUUSD is quoted.

Range ($)

Range = High − Low
// Full bar movement from lowest to highest tick. Best measure of how much the market moved in 1 hour.
Example: High = 2310.50, Low = 2297.80 → Range = $12.70. A $10 SL needs the expected range to be > $10 before taking a trade in that hour.

Body ($)

Body = |Close − Open|
// The actual committed move (candle body). Excludes wicks.

A large Range but small Body means the candle was whippy — the market moved a lot but rejected most of it. A large Body relative to Range means strong directional conviction.

Move % (of price)

Move% = (|Close − Open| / Open) × 100
// Body as a percentage of price. More comparable across time than raw $ because gold doubled since 2022.
Example: Open = 2300, Close = 2315 → Body = $15 → Move% = 0.65%.
This is used by the Regime Banner instead of raw $ range — fair comparison regardless of gold's price level.

Bull % (Directional Bias)

Bull% = (bars where Close > Open) / total bars × 100
// Bullish bar count as a percentage. 50% = neutral, >55% = bullish bias, <45% = bearish bias.
Example: Hour 21 (9 PM BKK) — sampled 207 times. 118 were bullish closes → Bull% = 57% → slight bullish bias during NY overlap.
Thresholds used by the dashboard: Bull > 55% → green BULL tag · Bear < 45% → red BEAR tag · 45–55% → blue NEUTRAL tag.

Avg Range — How the averages are computed

Avg Range (hour H) = mean(Range) for all bars where BKK_hour = H
// All-time simple mean. "Clean" version excludes thin-market and Monday-gap bars.

Two versions exist side-by-side in the hourly table:

  • Raw avg — includes every bar at that hour, including the gap/thin ones.
  • Clean avg — (shown in grey, in parentheses) — excludes thin-market (2–3 AM) and Monday-gap (Mon 5 AM) bars. Use this for actual trading decisions.

Signed Direction %

Direction% = (Close − Open) / Open × 100
// Positive = bullish bar, Negative = bearish bar. Used internally for regime comparison.

🎯 CONFIDENCE SCORE

Each stat has a Confidence % that reflects how much you should trust it. It is based purely on sample count — more historical bars at that exact DOW+hour slot = higher confidence.

Clean Samples (n)ConfidenceInterpretation
< 1010%Almost no data — ignore this slot
10 – 2935%Very small sample — treat as anecdote
30 – 5955%Moderate — useful directional hint only
60 – 11970%Good — reasonable to use for planning
120 – 24985%Strong — statistically reliable
≥ 25095%Very strong — use with confidence
Practical rule: For a DOW+hour slot that only exists once per week, 60 samples ≈ 60 weeks ≈ 1 year of data. You need at least 120 samples (2 years) before treating a bias as reliable.
Confidence does not mean the move will happen. It means "given enough history, this average is stable." Gold can still gap against any bias due to macro events.

📊 P-VALUE (STATISTICAL SIGNIFICANCE)

For each Top Slots row, a two-sided z-test for proportion tests whether the bull rate differs from coin-flip (50%). Defined in binom_p_value(k, n, p0=0.5) at app.py:103-108.

z = (k/n − 0.5) / √(0.5 × 0.5 / n)
p = erfc(|z| / √2)
// k = bullish bars, n = total bars at slot. Returns 1.0 if n < 5.

Star thresholds (used in Top Slots filter + display)

Symbolp-valueMeaning
★★p < 0.01Very strong — <1% chance the bias is random.
★p < 0.05Significant — standard academic threshold.
~p < 0.10Borderline — weak evidence.
nsp ≥ 0.10Not significant — treat as 50/50.

★ OPTIMAL EDGE SCORE (Heatmap mode)

Combined score that highlights cells where both the range and the directional bias are strong. Defined at app.py:190-192.

range_norm[d,h] = avg_range[d,h] / max(avg_range)
bias[d,h] = |bull_pct[d,h] − 50| / 50
optimal[d,h] = range_norm × bias × 100
// 0 = no edge (flat range OR coin flip); 100 = best possible cell

The score punishes cells with either a small range or a neutral bias. A cell only scores high if it has both a large range AND a strong directional tendency.

🌡 REGIME DELTA

Powers the colored Regime Banner on Overview. Defined inside compute_now() at app.py:348-361.

recent = df[ local_time ≥ (max_time − lookback_days) ]
recent_avg = mean( recent.move_pct )
hist_avg = mean( df.move_pct )
delta_pct = (recent_avg / hist_avg − 1) × 100
// Uses move_pct (% of price), not raw $ range — fair across years.

Banner thresholds (set in JS render)

  • 🔥 HOT: delta_pct > +25
  • 📊 NORMAL: −15 ≤ delta_pct ≤ +25
  • ❄️ COOL: delta_pct < −15

The lookback window is set in Settings → Regime Lookback (default 30 days). Note: the regime always uses the full historical dataset as the baseline, regardless of the Analysis Period filter.

POST /api/refresh

Runs fetch_data.py as a subprocess (timeout 120s) and rewrites the CSV. Implementation: app.py:456-466. Returns {ok: true, output: ...} on success or {error: ...} with HTTP 500 on failure (MT5 not running, symbol not in Market Watch, etc.).

This endpoint is invoked by:

  • Manual refresh — user clicks ⟳ REFRESH MT5 button.
  • Auto-refresh — every 60 seconds while AUTO ON is enabled. Silent (no UI toast) unless 2 consecutive failures occur.
